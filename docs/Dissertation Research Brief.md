# AI personalisation, customer trust and loyalty

**Daniel Christopher · Individual MSc dissertation · Submitted August 2026**

## Question
Among eligible UK online shoppers, how are perceived AI-driven personalisation, customer trust and loyalty intentions associated, and is the association statistically indirect through trust?

## Method
Quantitative cross-sectional Qualtrics questionnaire of 139 complete eligible UK responses, recruited by convenience and snowball sampling. SPSS reliability tests, correlations, HC3-robust regressions and PROCESS Model 4 mediation with 10,000 bootstrap samples. Python supported figures. No participant-level records are published.

## Aggregate findings
- Personalisation to trust: B = .575, p < .001.
- Trust to loyalty in joint model: B = .526, p < .001.
- Remaining direct personalisation coefficient: B = .048, p = .552 (not statistically significant).
- Indirect association through trust: .303; 95% bootstrap interval [.199, .422].
- Scales showed Cronbach's alpha from .809 to .859.

## Business implications
Evaluate relevance together with customer trust, transparency and control. Test changes experimentally and measure actual repurchase rather than treating a survey relationship as revenue uplift.

## Limits
Non-probability sample, self-reported perceptions and loyalty intentions; one time point. Associations do not prove causal mediation, represent every UK shopper, evaluate a retailer's algorithm or document achieved commercial impact.
